## Overview

GraphR² connects graph-task complexity to model and training design for generalizable and compositional graph reasoning. It stratifies graph tasks using massively parallel computation (MPC) round and memory complexity, relates these requirements to Transformer depth and context, and trains models through progressively more complex graph tasks. The accompanying GraphR²-Benchmark provides algorithm-grounded reasoning and reflective annotations.
